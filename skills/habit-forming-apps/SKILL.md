# Habit-forming app design

People return to products that reliably help them with something they already want, at the moment they want it. Design
for that; never for compulsion.

## 1. Find the habit worth building

Who is the user, what recurring need or feeling brings them (boredom, worry about money, wanting to learn, staying in
touch), how often it happens, and what they do today instead. A habit forms around a frequent need; a yearly need will
never be a daily app.

## 2. Design the loop

- **Trigger**: an external cue first (a notification, an e-mail, a message from a friend) that hands over to an internal
  one (the feeling itself). Each notification must carry value on its own.
- **Action**: the smallest step toward the reward. Remove every field, screen and tap that is not needed; one tap if
  possible.
- **Reward**: real value delivered fast, with some variety (new content, a result, a reply from people) so it stays
  interesting.
- **Investment**: something the user adds that makes the next visit better (preferences, content, progress, contacts),
  asked for after the reward, not before.

## 3. Onboarding

Get the user to their first real value (the "aha" moment) in the first session, ideally the first minute. Ask for
sign-up, permissions and details only when they become necessary, each with a reason.

## 4. Keep them

Progress that is visible (streaks with a forgiving freeze, levels, history), personalisation that improves with use,
social features where they help the core need, and a weekly summary of what they gained.

## 5. Measure and test

Day-1, day-7 and day-30 retention by cohort, time to first value, the share reaching the aha moment, notification
opt-out rate. Change one thing at a time and compare cohorts.

## Ethics (always)

Easy to leave, cancel and delete; no fake scarcity, hidden costs, guilt-tripping, endless feeds built to waste time, or
dark patterns; extra care with children, money and health. Ask whether the user would be glad they came back. When the
work is code, build it with Mode Codeur and check the flows in the browser.
