# Offer builder

A service is not an offer. "A marketing agency" is a service; **"10 qualified sales calls in 30 days with our AI
prospecting system, and you only pay for the calls that show up"** is an offer: a stranger understands it in one second.

## 1. Find where the person wins

Ask about three things and look for the overlap:

- **Pain**: problems they have lived through and solved (slow follow-up of leads, admin overload, empty classes…).
- **Profession**: industries they know from the inside (their own jobs, their family's, what they studied).
- **Passion**: what they enjoy doing and talking about.

Prefer a market they understand over a market that looks rich. A former nurse who likes teaching sells AI training to
clinics better than a CRM to roofers.

## 2. Sell one of the five things cold buyers want

More money, lower costs, more leads or clients, more status, or something hot right now. "Saves time" is weak on its own:
translate it into money.

## 3. Build the one line, in four parts

1. **Specific outcome**: a number, not "more" (10 qualified calls, 30 new reviews, 20 % fewer no-shows).
2. **Time frame**: when they get it (in 30 days).
3. **Method**: what is special about how (with our AI prospecting system).
4. **Risk reversal**: a guarantee, pay on results, or a small fee plus a fee per result. A beginner should carry the
   risk: it is what makes a stranger say yes.

Turn the four dials up: new money rather than optimisation, done for them rather than taught, as little risk and as
little commitment (month to month) as possible.

## 4. Price it and test it

- Price against the value: ask what one new client is worth to them; the offer should return at least five times its cost.
- Give two shapes: a monthly fee, and a lower fee plus a fee per result.
- Write the pitch line, a one-paragraph version and the three questions to qualify a prospect.
- It does not need to be perfect: it needs to be tested this week. The real offer comes out of conversations with
  prospects; revise it after every five conversations.

Save it to `<sharedBotFolder>/business/offer.md`. Never claim results the person has not had.
