# Client outreach

Distribution comes from four places: ads, content, warm outreach and cold outreach. Without money or an audience, the
last two are the fastest. Pick the path that fits the person:

## Path 1: no money, no experience: the warm network

Their first clients are often already in their phone. Build the list with them (contacts, WhatsApp, Facebook,
Instagram, LinkedIn, e-mails of the last two years) into `<sharedBotFolder>/business/prospects.csv`. Then a normal
conversation first (news, family, work), and only when it fits, a **referral ask**, never a pitch:

> "By the way, do you know anyone who struggles with <problem>? I'm taking on five businesses to get <outcome> in
> <time frame>, free, in exchange for honest feedback and a testimonial."

Asking whether they know someone removes the awkwardness; often the answer is "actually, me".

## Path 2: little money: the interview method

Invite people in the niche to a short interview or podcast about their work: they get visibility and clips they can
post; the person learns their exact problems in their own words. At the end: "You mentioned <problem>. That's what I
help businesses like yours with. Would it be a bad idea to book another call where I show you how?" Interviews can be
recorded and transcribed with `transcribe_audio` to pull the words that go into the offer.

A daily mix of about 100 actions: proposals on freelance sites (people there already want help: answer their problem,
no podcast), voice notes or personal messages on LinkedIn, e-mails, and DMs, follow-ups included.

## Path 3: some money: cold e-mail at volume

Work backwards with the solo-service-business numbers. Volume needs a market of hundreds of thousands of businesses,
separate sending domains and warmed inboxes (two weeks before sending), and a sending tool; cold e-mail rules differ by
country (consent, opt-out, sender identity): check them and always include who is writing and how to stop.

## Writing messages

Short, about them, one question at the end. Personalise the first line from something real (their site, a post, a
review). Never fake familiarity, never mass-send from the person's own mailbox, and send only what the person approved
(through the connected Gmail, Outlook, LinkedIn or WhatsApp when they say so). Log every message and reply in the
prospect list (name, channel, date, status, next follow-up) and follow up 3 times, 3 to 5 days apart, before stopping.
