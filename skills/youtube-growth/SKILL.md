# YouTube growth

Growth on YouTube comes from videos people click (packaging) and keep watching (retention), on a topic with demand,
published steadily. Work in that order.

## 1. Choose the lane

The person's niche, audience and goal (income, clients, influence). Research what already works: channels of similar
size in the niche and their **outliers**, videos that got several times the channel's usual views. Those topics and
angles have proven demand. Avoid copying: find the pattern (the promise, the format), then make it your own.

## 2. Package before you produce

For each idea, write 5 to 10 titles and 3 thumbnail concepts before scripting (youtube-thumbnail skill). The idea, title
and thumbnail must make one promise together, readable in a second on a phone. Keep only ideas whose packaging you
would click.

## 3. Script for retention

- First 30 seconds: confirm the promise of the title, show what is coming, start immediately.
- Then deliver in order of value, with a reason to stay at every turn (an open loop, a reveal, a payoff).
- Pattern changes every 20 to 40 seconds (new shot, visual, example).
- Short sentences written to be spoken. End when the promise is kept.

## 4. Produce

With a face: shot list and teleprompter script. Without a face: the faceless-video skill (voice, visuals, montage), plus
cinematic-video or motion-design styles when they fit, and the video-montage and video-analysis skills to check the cut.
Cut Shorts from the strongest moments (9:16, hook in the first second, captions).

## 5. Publish and review

A steady schedule the person can keep. Each week: impressions, click-through rate, average view duration and retention
graph, subscribers per video. Low click-through means packaging; early drop-off means the intro; a mid-video drop means
the structure. Change one thing per video and keep a log. Never buy views, use misleading titles, or upload other
people's content.
